Hot water systems for Brisbane's southside: choosing and installing new

Choosing new hot water systems on Brisbane's southside comes down to four things: the existing installation, how many people draw on it, the tariff and the roof, and the bills you are prepared to carry. Moyle Plumbing & Gasfitting supplies and installs electric, gas, heat pump and solar systems from Yatala. Repairs on existing units are covered on the hot water repairs page.

Comparing the options

SystemSuitsConsider
Electric storageLike-for-like swaps, off-peak tariffs, tight budgetsHighest running cost on a standard tariff
Heat pumpMost southside homes with an outdoor spot and airflowFan noise near bedrooms or neighbours; rebates change
Gas storageHomes with natural gas and steady demandNeeds flue clearance and a drain for the relief line
Continuous flow gasLarge households, townhouses short on spaceGas line must be sized for it; power point for ignition
Solar with boosterUnshaded north-facing roof, long-term ownersBiggest upfront cost; roof structure and access

Sizing matters as much as type. A family with teenagers and two bathrooms needs a different unit from a couple in a townhouse, and an undersized system is the most common reason people are unhappy with a new install.

What the installation includes

  • Removal and disposal of the old unit.
  • New isolation valve, pressure limiting valve and non-return valve where required.
  • Tempering valve on the bathroom supply, set and recorded.
  • Relief valve drain run to a safe discharge point.
  • Electrical or gas connection by the appropriately licensed trade, with gas work tested and certified.
  • A base or bracket that keeps the unit off wet ground.
  • Commissioning, a check of temperature at the taps, and a run-through of the controls with you.

The figure is fixed before the day and covers all of the above. If a change of location or fuel type is involved, it is scoped and priced in advance.

Southside houses and what fits

Brick homes across Eight Mile Plains, Rochedale South, Springwood, Underwood and Kuraby typically have an electric storage unit on a side wall and space to put a heat pump in its place, which is why heat pumps have overtaken everything else as the southside upgrade. Townhouses and units frequently lack the outdoor room, so there a compact electric tank or gas instantaneous unit is the workable answer. Streets with natural gas open up gas storage and continuous flow, while properties on LPG need the cylinder installation checked for capacity first.

Frequently asked questions

Which size hot water system do I need?

It depends on the number of people, bathrooms and how hot water is used through the day. Tell us the household and we will recommend a capacity rather than simply matching the old unit, which may have been wrong to begin with.

Are there rebates for heat pumps on the southside?

Schemes come and go at state and federal level and through energy retailers. We tell you what applies at the time of quoting and how it affects the price.

Can you install a new system the same day the old one fails?

For a common storage unit going in where the old one was, often yes. Heat pumps, solar and relocations are booked as planned work.

Do I need an electrician as well?

For a like-for-like electric unit the existing connection is usually reused by the licensed trade. A heat pump or a change of unit type may need electrical work, which we arrange as part of the job.
