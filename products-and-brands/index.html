Products and brands

Plumbing and hot water brands we supply and service

The plumbing and hot water brands in a typical Gold Coast house are a mix of what the builder fitted, what the last owner chose and what was on special at the time. Moyle Plumbing & Gasfitting works on all of the major brands sold in Australia, carries common parts for them, and supplies new products from manufacturers whose parts and support we can rely on.

Hot water systems

Storage, continuous flow, heat pump and solar units from the major Australian and imported makers. On a repair we identify the model from its plate, carry elements, thermostats and valves that fit most units, and order model-specific parts where needed. On a replacement we recommend on capacity, warranty, parts availability and the fit for your home, and explain why. Choices are listed on the hot water page.

Tapware and mixers

Ceramic cartridges, spindles and seals differ by brand and even by model year. We identify what you have before opening the wall, so premium and imported mixers common in Hope Island and renovated homes are repaired with the right part rather than a substitute that fails early.

Toilet suites

Inlet valves, outlet washers, buttons and seals for the common suites are carried on the van. When a suite is replaced we match the outlet position and choose a dual-flush model with a good water rating; see toilets.

Kitchen appliances and filtration

  • InSinkErator food waste disposers, installed and replaced.
  • Dishwashers of any brand, connected to water and drainage.
  • Water filters: under-sink, whole-house, boiling and chilled units, and tank water treatment.

Valves, pumps and gas

Pressure limiting valves, tempering valves, backflow devices, tank pressure pumps, sewage pumps, regulators and bayonet fittings from the established manufacturers, chosen for parts availability and compliance. See pumps and gas fitting.

How we choose what to supply

  1. Parts must be obtainable. A product nobody can get parts for in five years is a bad buy however cheap it is.
  2. The warranty must be honoured locally. We favour brands with service networks in South East Queensland.
  3. It must suit the water and the pressure. Some products do not cope with the high mains pressure in northern Gold Coast estates without a limiting valve.
  4. It must meet Australian standards. Plumbing products need WaterMark certification; gas appliances need Australian gas certification. We do not install products without them.

If you already have a product in mind, tell us and we will say whether it is a sound choice for your house. Whatever we install carries a firm price before the work starts. When the product that has failed is flooding the laundry, shut things off first using the emergency plumbing checklist steps. Frequently asked questions

Can I supply my own tapware or hot water unit?

Yes, provided it carries the required Australian certification. We check it suits your pressure and the existing installation before fitting, and tell you if it will not.

Do you carry parts for older hot water units?

Elements, thermostats and valves are largely standardised and we carry them. Brand-specific parts for older units are ordered where still made; where they are not, we tell you so before you spend money on a repair.

Which brand of hot water system is best?

There is no single answer. The right unit depends on fuel, household size, location and budget. We recommend a specific model for your situation and explain why, rather than pushing one brand.
