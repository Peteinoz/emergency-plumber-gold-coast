Hot water Springwood: repairs and replacement

Hot water in Springwood mostly means an electric storage unit on the side of a 1970s or 80s house, on its second or third replacement. Moyle Plumbing & Gasfitting repairs those units, replaces them when the tank has gone, and installs heat pumps where the running-cost saving makes sense, all from Yatala with the cost settled first.

Is it time to replace?

  • Water is weeping from the seam or base of the tank, not from a valve.
  • Rust streaks down the tank or rusty water from the hot taps.
  • It has needed more than one repair in the last couple of years.
  • The unit is well past ten years old and a major part has failed.
  • The household has grown and it runs out every morning.
  • It is on a standard tariff and the electricity bill is the problem you are trying to fix.

If none of those apply and the failure is just an element, thermostat or valve, repairing is the sensible choice and we will say so. Diagnosis comes first; the set price for repair or replacement follows.

Springwood's housing and its hot water

The suburb, with Underwood, Daisy Hill and Slacks Creek around it, was largely built in the 1970s and 1980s. Electric storage units on side walls are the norm, often on an off-peak tariff with a timer. Some streets have natural gas; many do not. The lowset brick homes have space for a heat pump where the old unit sat, and that has become the usual upgrade around here, because the running-cost saving on a standard tariff is significant and the outdoor spot usually has the airflow the unit needs.

Older units also predate some current requirements. Replacing one brings the installation up to date: a tempering valve for the bathrooms, a correct relief drain, limiting and non-return valves, and a proper base.

Replacement day

  1. Old unit isolated, drained and removed.
  2. New unit set on a base, connected with new valves and a tempering valve.
  3. Electrical connection made by the licensed trade; gas tested and certified if applicable.
  4. Filled, purged, heated and checked at the taps.
  5. Old unit taken away, area left clean.

A plain electric swap is usually finished in a day. Heat pumps and gas conversions are scheduled in.

Frequently asked questions

Can you replace my Springwood hot water unit today?

For a standard electric storage swap, usually yes if you ring early enough in the day. We confirm the size on the phone and bring the unit with us.

Will a heat pump fit where my electric unit is?

Most side-wall locations on Springwood's lowset homes suit a heat pump, provided there is airflow around it and it is not directly outside a bedroom window. We check on site.

Do I have to change the tariff for a heat pump?

Not necessarily. Heat pumps can run on a standard tariff and still cost far less than a plain electric unit. Some tariffs and controllers let you run it in the daytime to use solar power if you have it.

Why is my new unit dripping from the side pipe?

A small discharge from the relief valve while heating is normal. A constant stream is not, and we would come back and check it.
